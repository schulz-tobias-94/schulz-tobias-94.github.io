About

I am an astroparticle physicist specializing in data analysis, simulation, and computational science. My work focuses on detector response, signal processing, statistical methods, and simulation models within large international research collaborations.

I currently work within the Pierre Auger Collaboration, including detector operations, photomultiplier signal analysis, baseline reconstruction algorithms, the muon deficit, the neutron component in extensive air showers, scintillator detectors, and detector simulations.

I am interested in interdisciplinary work at the intersection of science, technology, and data-driven applications.

Current Focus

Large-scale simulations

Detector and air shower simulations using distributed batch workflows and HTCondor.

Data analysis & visualization

Processing, analysis, and visualization of simulation and experimental datasets using Python, NumPy, pandas, and matplotlib.

Detector response studies

Comparison of detector simulations with experimental measurements to study detector response and air shower observables.
